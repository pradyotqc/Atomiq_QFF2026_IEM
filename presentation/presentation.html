Stage 1 Methodology: Anti-Leakage Firewall & Feature Engineering

Slide 3 / 14 · Stage 1 Pipeline

Zero-Leakage Discipline (CV_SPLIT_SEED = 42)

  • Training Imbalance: Stage1_Train has 300 labeled rows (35 yes / 265 no = 11.67% base positive rate).
  • Post-Call Purge: duration is strictly dropped across both train and test datasets before any engineering.
  • Single Blind Pass: Stage1_Test (100 rows) is never consulted for imputation, feature selection, scaling, or calibration; scored once with ascending customer_id tie-breaks.

Fold-Isolated Transformation Stages

  • Domain Features: Engineered contact intensity, previous-campaign outcome ratios, macroeconomic interactions, and balance brackets.
  • Strict Per-Fold Fit: SimpleImputer, OneHotEncoder, StandardScaler, SelectKBest(f_classif, k=6), and RKHS eigenbasis projection fit inside each training fold only.
  • Platt Calibration: Inner 5-fold sigmoid scaling aligns predicted probabilities before Stages 2 & 3 use them in revenue expectations.
Why Calibration Matters Downstream: Stage 2 computes p_i * deposit_i - cost_i, and Stage 3 multiplies six customer probabilities. Overconfident rankings distort budget allocation even if ROC-AUC appears unchanged.

Stage 1 Quantum Core: Solving Kernel Concentration

Slide 4 / 14 · Quantum Kernel

Diagnosis: Default zz_feature_map Collapse

  • Qiskit's default zz_feature_map encodes offsets as 2(π - x_i)(π - x_j).
  • Across 6 standardized banking covariates, statevectors spread near-orthogonally — collapsing off-diagonal kernel entries toward 0 and degrading 5-fold CV to near random (PR-AUC = 0.1391, ROC-AUC = 0.5145).

Zero-Centered Pauli Kernel + Spectral Whitening

  • Mapping bounded b * tanh(z/2) into zero-centered pauli_feature_map(6, reps=1, paulis=['Z','ZZ']) keeps phases proportional to x_i and x_i x_j, eliminating 2π² scrambling around origin.
  • Fold-isolated spectral whitening F = K V Λ^(-1/2) + L2 LogReg lifts performance to PR-AUC = 0.2406, Brier = 0.0993, and ECE = 0.0043.
Aer Kernel Simulation & Noise Audit (ComputeUncompute, 12-Customer Subsample, 2,048 Shots): Finite-shot Aer matches statevector at MAE = 0.0052; synthetic 0.1% 1Q / 1.0% 2Q depolarizing + readout noise raises unmitigated MAE to 0.1574 (simulator check, not physical QPU).

Stage 2 Formulation: 20q QUBO Ising & 10q Slack-Free XY-QAOA

Slide 7 / 14 · Stage 2 Math
Constrained Objective: Maximize Σ (p_i d_i - c_i) x_i over Top-10 subject to Σ x_i = 6, budget Σ c_i x_i ≤ 25, capacity Σ w_i x_i ≤ 22.

Path A: 20q QUBO + Exact 220 Ising Verification

  • QuadraticProgramToQubo(penalty=36.0) maps 10 decisions + 10 integer slack qubits into 185 Pauli terms (ising_offset = 4484.7345); $\lambda = 36.0 > \sum |v_i|$ ensures constraint violations cost more than any objective gain.
  • Exhaustive 220 scan confirms exact Ising ground state matches classical optimum (-23.183526225086 vs -23.183526225087, match=True).

Path B: 10q Dicke |D106> XY-QAOA & Aer HW-VQE

  • Brickwork RXX(β)RYY(β) mixer (p=2) initialized in symmetric Dicke state |D_10^6> commutes with $\sum Z_i$, preserving K=6 at 100.00% feasible mass with zero slack qubits.
  • 10q HW-VQE ansatz (RealAmplitudes r=1, CVaR α=0.15) concentrates 16.42% (SV) / 16.80% (4k shots) / 15.42% (mitigated Aer noise) onto 1110001110.

Circuit Realization, Equivalence & Confusion Mitigation

  • |q5 q4 q3 q2 q1 q0> = |C0206 C0349 C0327 C0079 C0031 C0081>; each qubit uses Ry(2 arcsin sqrt(p_j)) (depth 2, 0 CNOTs, 8,192 Aer shots).
  • Bit Ordering: Bitstring b = b5 b4 b3 b2 b1 b0 reads qubit j at MSB0 index 5 - j.
  • Exact DP Check (No Speedup Claim): Mathematically equivalent to Poisson-Binomial DP (0.28204944, exact within 1e-16); built for circuit realization & noise mitigation benchmarking (expected subscribers = 1.8970).
  • Why Readout Inversion Works: 1.5% 0→1 asymmetric flips push dense K=2 (33.8%) over K≥3; Kronecker inverse $\bigotimes M_j^{-1}$ removes 95.9% of that bias.
